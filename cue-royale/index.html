Privacy Policy

8 Ball Pool: Cue Royale · Developed by Wrexa Technologies

Last updated: 11 November 2026

No ads · No account · No tracking

This Privacy Policy explains how Wrexa Technologies (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile game 8 Ball Pool: Cue Royale (the “Game”). By downloading or using the Game, you agree to this policy.

The short version

The Game shows no advertisements and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or any other directly identifying personal information. Your game progress and purchases are stored on your own device. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to connect you to an opponent when you choose to play online.

Information stored on your device

  • Game data. Your settings, selected cue and table, tutorial progress, local stats, and your unlock status are saved locally on your device only. We cannot read this data, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Online multiplayer

The Game includes an optional online multiplayer mode. It is used only when you tap to play online; the rest of the Game works fully offline against the computer.

  • Display name. When you play online you may provide a display name. This name is shown to the opponent you are matched with. If you do not set one, a generic name is used. We do not require the name to be your real name.
  • Match relay. While an online match is in progress, your shots and any in-game chat are sent through our relay server (wrexa-cueroyale.fly.dev) and forwarded in real time to the other player. The relay passes these messages between the two players so the match stays in sync.
  • No permanent storage. The relay does not create a profile for you, does not require sign-in, and does not permanently store your display name, shots, or chat. These messages exist only for the duration of the match and are not retained after it ends.
  • Technical connection data. As with any internet service, the server temporarily processes connection information such as your IP address to route match traffic. This is used only to operate the connection and is not used to identify or profile you.

Purchases

The Game offers a single optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks all cues and tables. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment-card details. Google processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy

The Game records only whether the unlock has been purchased, so your paid content stays available. You can restore a previous purchase from within the Game.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or any ad network.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Permissions the Game uses

  • Internet / Network state — required for online multiplayer and to process in-app purchases. The Game is otherwise fully playable offline.
  • Vibration — used for in-game haptic feedback.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience. Because it contains no ads and no behavioural tracking, it does not collect personal information for advertising or profiling from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention & security

Game data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. Online-match messages are transient and are not retained after the match ends. We apply reasonable measures to protect information in transit, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International users

The Game is available globally. When you play online, match-relay traffic may be processed on servers located outside your own country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
